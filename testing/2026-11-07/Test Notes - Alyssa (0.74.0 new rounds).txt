SURVIVAL MODE 0.74.0 - WHAT TO TEST (ALYSSA)
Build: AC8SurvivalMode_COOP2MP_0.74.0_TESTER_Online_COOP_ZFA44.zip (the keys are in the Key List file)
Everything here is new and has NOT been in the game yet. Solo first, then co-op. After each session please send the
whole ue4ss\Mods\AC8SurvivalMode folder's logs (AC8SurvivalMode_lua.log, AC8SurvivalMode.log, the crumbs files) and
ue4ss\UE4SS.log, plus any crash folder. A note of the time something odd happened helps a lot.

1. FIRST: the quick tests (solo, any round, in the break is easiest)
   a. Ctrl+F10 (fishing TEST: only the shoals and the boat, no story, no subtitles, no clock): 8 shoals boxed round
      SAL's boat. Can you lock a shoal? Does one missile kill it? The gun? A bomb? A splash when it dies? Press
      Ctrl+F10 again (spheres off), spawn again (Ctrl+F10 x3 then x1) and try the gun again.
      (Done in your first test: they spawned, one hit each, the splash played. Thank you!)
   b. Ctrl+F6 five times, a few seconds apart: does the sun move to sunset (2), stay (3), go to night with stars (4),
      and come back to normal (5)? Screenshots of 2 and 4 please.
   c. Ctrl+F12: four cruise missiles from the north: boxed? lockable? one missile each? Does the air raid siren
      play at the airfield and stop after 20 s? Let one reach the runway.
   d. Ctrl+F5: CHARLENE lands? Watch where she touches down, whether she bounces, pulls up, or stops on the runway.
   e. Ctrl+9 on an enemy: is it the ZFA-44 (black and grey, red and white fins)? Right way round? Burners at the back?
      Is the laser pod on its back painted (not black, not the body's pattern smeared on it)? Ctrl+9 again on other
      enemies for paints 2, 3 and 4: which one looks right? (Paint 4 should give the pod a metal shine.)
   f. Ctrl+= (the Spear at you): fly straight once, then turning: does a rod come down? Damage? Ctrl+BACKSPACE: a UAV
      locks you (a laser?), kill it inside 8 s (it should say STRIKE CANCELLED), then again and let it strike.
   g. Ctrl+, (storm): does lightning hit you about every 10 s? The HUD should black out for 2 s, a noise, a shake,
      NO damage. Press again: does the 3 s "stall" do anything to your plane?

2. THE ROUNDS (solo)
   - GONE FISHIN' (Ctrl+HOME: THIS is the real round with SAL's subtitles; press it in the break between rounds):
     SAL and COMONA CONTROL talk on the subtitle strip, then 48 shoals all round SAL's boat and 3 minutes on the
     clock, catches pop up as you kill shoals, the weigh-in card at the end (grade, pay). Is the pay fair? Does the
     sky start getting later each round after it?
   - Ctrl+PAGE_UP then play THE FESTIVAL: the thanks, the siren, searchlights, Tu-160s from three sides, missiles
     to shoot down. Each formation keeps launching salvos until it's shot down. Does it end when the bombers are
     down? Is it too hard / too easy?
   - Ctrl+PAGE_DOWN a few times: night (can you still see the HUD, the airfield, targets?), then sunrise, midday.
   - Ctrl+0 THE ESCORT: CHARLENE and her escorts from the far south-west, enemies after her: a wave of 4-12
     fighters about every 45 s, each from a different direction, until she touches down. Do they all land? Does
     the round end? Then the STORE's AIRFIELD page: VAMPIRES. Do ZFA-44s come out of the ship's
     containers (or appear above it)? Do they fight?
   - Ctrl+- SHOOT DOWN THE RECON DRONE: the recon drone up north, then the trap: UAVs lock you, 8 s to kill
     each; a Spear strike every 10 s while they're up, some miss; a hit takes half your health (a second one
     with half or less left shoots you down); the drone's own strike on the airfield costs it 40 HP.
   - Ctrl+. IFF FATAL ERROR: the A-10, its storm blacking out your HUD near it.

3. CO-OP (host + joiners): the same rounds once each. Watch for: every player seeing the same sky; each player's own
   fishing haul on the card; nobody losing kg when SOMEONE ELSE hits SAL's boat; the ZFA-44s on a joiner's screen;
   the Spear only hitting the locked player; anything that stalls a round.

4. ALWAYS: a crash, a freeze, a round that never ends, a stutter (when?), anything in the wrong place.
