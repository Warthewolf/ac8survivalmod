AC8 Survival Mode COOP2MP 0.73.81 (0.73.81-coop2mp.82) - change log
====================================================================
Since 0.73.80 (CHANGELOG_0.73.80.txt). Patch over a 0.73.78 COOP2MP install (with or without the 0.73.79 / 0.73.80
patches); everyone in a co-op session needs 0.73.81.

- Spaceport Assault: every WASP you sink sends 4 TU-160s at the airfield 6 s later ("TU-160S INBOUND / FROM THE NORTH"
  or "SOUTH": the groups come from the north and the south in turn). They fly in over the runway and bomb it, show on
  the minimap and pay as heavy bombers, and the round ends only once they're down too. The game has one 4-bomber flight,
  so if another Wasp goes down while 4 Tu-160s are still up, the next 4 come as soon as those are shot down. None in the
  retake or once the spaceport is captured (and a capture sends the ones up home with the fighters). Settings:
  [specials.spaceport.tu160] in survival.ini (group size, delay, directions).
- Heavy bombers (any bomber raid): when a Tu-95 / Tu-160 flight's lead is shot down first, the next one now takes over
  its bombing route every time (it could be lost right after the flight appeared, and the rest never bombed).

Still with the next full build (runtime 0.32.24): the native log's lag fix and other players' weapon stand-ins.
4 Tu-160s for EVERY Wasp at once (up to 16 in the air) needs three more 4-bomber flights in the game files: a full build.
