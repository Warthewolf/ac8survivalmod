AC8 Survival Mode COOP2MP 0.73.83 (0.73.83-coop2mp.84) - change log
====================================================================
Since 0.73.82 (CHANGELOG_0.73.82.txt). Patch over a 0.73.78 COOP2MP install (with or without the 0.73.79 - 0.73.82
patches); everyone in a co-op session needs 0.73.83.

Co-op
- A player who leaves and comes back: their old plane's radar blip is gone now, not only the plane. Any teammate's
  plane hidden on your screen (they left, they're DOWN, they're not in the mission) is off your radar too, and back on
  it when they're back.
- After the Alicorn round, new enemies sometimes weren't linked between the games: joiners saw extra enemies that
  couldn't be shot down, copies were torn down as "strays", and every joiner resynced every few minutes to the end of
  the run (Rapt0r's "desync" in the 32-round run). Fixed.
- VOID WATCHER 1 going home no longer leaves a frozen copy flying on the joiners' screens for minutes.
- "ROUND N KILLS / YOU N": each player now sees their own kills, not the host's.
- Host page: when CO-OP ENEMIES isn't the default (+15% each), the row says so and START RUN reads "UNRANKED": the co-op
  leaderboard only takes runs on the default room rules (Shazzy's 0.73.82 run wasn't sent for that, with no warning).

Lag late in long runs
- Survival asked the game "is this unit still alive?" far more often than it needed to, and each ask got slower with
  every unit spawned in the run (about 7 ms an ask by round 25). The allies, the HUD, the director and the "left the
  mission" check now reuse recent answers.
- A special's units (the Spaceport's 27 ships and its landing-craft waves, the amphibious craft, a boss that's down)
  are taken off the game's unit list once the round's over, so every later ask stays cheap. They come off one every
  half second, starting 15 s after the round, so nothing piles up.
- An airfield defence or allied ship lost is now noticed within about 4 s (it was 2 s).

Leaderboard
- VALKA wins now count on the server (the board scored runs with her 2,250 - 4,500 below what the game showed). Needs
  the Worker redeployed; runs already stored keep their score.

Still waiting on the next full build (runtime 0.32.24): the native log's lag fix and other players' weapon stand-ins.
Still being looked at (from the logs of 2026-11-04): ammo not readable on Casual, SECOND CHANCE's floor in solo after
an airfield stop, a restart's fake "round 1 clear", enemies chasing a player who left.
