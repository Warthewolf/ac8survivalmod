AC8 Survival Mode COOP2MP 0.73.84 (0.73.84-coop2mp.85) - change log
====================================================================
Since 0.73.83 (CHANGELOG_0.73.83.txt). Patch over a 0.73.78 COOP2MP install (with or without the 0.73.79 - 0.73.83
patches); everyone in a co-op session needs 0.73.84.

- Crash on quit fixed (Shazzy, 2026-11-04, round 24): with the GUN upgrade bought, quitting the mission could crash the
  game. Survival kept the gun's settings object to raise its damage, and on the way out it put the stock damage back
  into it, but by then the game had sometimes already freed that object with the level, and the write crashed. Most
  quits got there first, which is why it only crashed sometimes. Survival now only touches it while the game still
  holds it, and remembers the gun's stock damage for the whole session, so an upgrade never carries into the next run.
- The same guard for two other things kept between levels: VALKA's red sky (put back at the end of her fight) and the
  Ladon missile's settings (its damage and turn rate are toned down from them).
