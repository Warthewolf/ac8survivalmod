AC8 Survival Mode COOP2MP 0.73.79 (0.73.79-coop2mp.80) - change log
====================================================================
Since 0.73.78. Patch over a 0.73.78 COOP2MP install; everyone in a co-op session needs 0.73.79.

LESS LAG AND STUTTER (no gameplay changes)
- Survival no longer searches the game's whole object list over and over. Lookups it needs all the time are now kept,
  which cuts the steady cost behind the slight choppiness during play.
- The Alicorn: no more big freeze when it appears, and no more stutter every 2 seconds during its fight.
- The Ladons: their missiles are checked once per level instead of every 10 seconds (a small hitch every 10 s is gone).
- VALKA: her song and railgun sounds now load in the break before her round, not mid-fight (two short freezes gone).
- Round clears: no slow airfield-defence search when you haven't bought any defences.
- Shuttle round: one search at take-off instead of three.
- Menus: Survival's menu checks run much less often during a run, and the main menu's code rests while you fly.
- Smaller savings: your position, the level-exit check, Wolf traps, the difficulty setting and co-op's pause check all
  cost less each update.

FIXES
- The Alicorn no longer dives once all four ballast tanks are destroyed (if it's under water then, it surfaces).
- Co-op: joiners can finish enemies with their missiles again (before, enemies sat at 1 HP until the host killed them).
- Co-op: hits are no longer lost when the host's health update arrives at the same moment, and enemies no longer flicker
  back to full health on a joiner's screen after each hit.

NOTES
- Version 0.73.79-coop2mp.80: a player still on 0.73.78 can't join (or host for) a 0.73.79 player.
- Leaderboard: runs show NOT ACCEPTED until the new build is registered.
- Coming with the next full build: one more stutter fix in the native runtime (its log no longer waits for the disk on
  every line, which caused short freezes at round starts and clears).
- Testers: the TESTER patch keeps the same test keys as 0.73.78.
